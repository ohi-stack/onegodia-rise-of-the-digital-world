# Stamford Station / Washington Boulevard Visual Reference Pass

**Project:** Onegodia: Rise of the Digital World™  
**Purpose:** Browser-game and Unreal world-building reference  
**Status:** Reference observations only — not surveyed geometry  
**Canonical player origin:** Stamford Hospital

## Reference Area

This pass documents visual and spatial cues supplied for the Stamford corridor around:

- Stamford Station
- Washington Boulevard
- 430 Washington Boulevard
- South State Street / rail approach
- the rail bridge / underpass condition
- the Harbor Point direction south of the station

The supplied reference images are used as design guidance. They are not themselves redistributed as game assets, and this document does not claim survey-grade positional accuracy.

## Observed Urban Features

### Stamford Station

- Major rail/transit anchor immediately adjacent to the Washington Boulevard corridor.
- Elevated rail infrastructure and bridge/underpass conditions are visually prominent.
- The station should become a major navigation checkpoint between Downtown Stamford and Harbor Point.
- Rail structures, overhead infrastructure, retaining walls, and transit-adjacent road geometry should be represented in the high-fidelity Unreal build.

### Washington Boulevard

- Multi-lane urban roadway with strong visual relationship to rail infrastructure.
- Signalized intersections and pedestrian crossings are important to the visual identity of the corridor.
- The approach includes underpass/bridge conditions that should affect lighting, traffic flow, collision, and sound design.
- Road markings, directional arrows, curbs, streetlights, utility infrastructure, and construction details are useful environmental cues.

### 430 Washington Boulevard

- Large contemporary glass office structure visible from the rail/station corridor.
- Useful as a skyline and navigation anchor in the Stamford Station district.
- Early builds may use a massing proxy; the final landmark treatment should be separately reviewed before claiming exact architectural recreation.

### Parking / Service Structures

- Structured parking and service/construction areas are visible around the station corridor.
- These elements help distinguish the area from a generic downtown street kit.
- Include ramps, garage massing, fencing, barriers, utility/service zones, and loading access where appropriate.

### Harbor Point Relationship

- Harbor Point is south of the rail/station zone and functions naturally as the next player-facing expansion destination.
- The browser route should treat Stamford Station as an intermediate checkpoint, not the final destination.

## Browser Game Route

Current abstract route:

**Stamford Hospital → Vehicle Staging → Washington Boulevard → Stamford Station → Harbor Point**

The browser map is intentionally schematic. It should communicate real landmark relationships without implying survey accuracy.

## Unreal Environment Priorities

1. Preserve Stamford Hospital as the canonical PlayerStart.
2. Build the road corridor toward Washington Boulevard.
3. Establish the Stamford Station rail/underpass environment.
4. Add 430 Washington Boulevard as a massing/navigation anchor.
5. Add structured parking/service context.
6. Validate pedestrian crossings, sidewalks, and vehicle collision.
7. Continue the playable route south toward Harbor Point.
8. Add traffic, pedestrians, lighting, sound, and World Partition validation after the route is stable.

## Asset / Legal Rule

Use the visual references to understand place character and spatial cues. Do not copy proprietary map imagery, Street View overlays, UI elements, or third-party photographs directly into the commercial game without a separately documented license.
